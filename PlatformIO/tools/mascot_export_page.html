Meet Rev

One character, drawn the way your reference clip is drawn: a white silhouette with nothing inside it, a glow in the theme colour, and sticker-pack timing. The tuft on its head is a gauge needle. It droops when Rev's asleep, swings with the reading and pins at redline, so it works on any car. A Haldex clutch-ring collar is an optional extra for cars that have one.

Screensaver loop

About twelve seconds. A slow idle, then a gag: slam, laugh, keel over, pop up, run off, cut to black. Tap it to startle it.

Boost

Rev is the boost gauge. Vacuum sucks it thin, boost blows it up. Drop the slider fast for the blow-off.

RPM

Rev jogs with the engine. Idle is a shuffle, 3,000 is a run, 5,000 is a sprint, and the limiter bounces it off the ring. The quiff is the needle.

900 rpm

Ignition off / on

Key off: a wave goodbye, a yawn, lies down, zzz, and the glass fades out. Key on: springs up, the quiff does a cluster-style sweep, then idle.

Row wave

Three gauges side by side at key-off: the first two turn to each other and wave, then the second and third, while the others watch the wave go by. Then all three wave to you together.

Haldex

Lock is effort. Open, Rev is loafing. As the coupling closes it plants its feet, digs in and grits, and at full lock the ring lights and it shouts LOCK.

Character sheet

Same face, three body proportions. Pick the row that feels right. The expressions are the ones the gauge needs: content, dizzy for redline, laughing, startled, sleeping, cold, hot, gritted for lock, and knocked out.

What changed from the first pass

  • No interior detail. Eyes and mouth are black cut-outs in a white shape. Everything else is silhouette.
  • Glow instead of shading. The only colour is the theme colour, as a bloom around the edges and on the words. Red and ice blue tint the glow for hot and cold.
  • A little person, not a blob. Round head, short torso, stub legs and mitten hands, like the reference. Two-thirds head. Expressions carry the character, not props.
  • Sticker timing. Poses are held and snapped between, with squash on landing. Beats are separated by cuts to black.
  • On the gauge: each pose is a sprite with two masks, white and glow, so the theme colour is applied at draw time and one file set covers every theme.
